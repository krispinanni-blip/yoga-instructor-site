About Maya

10+ years of practice, one simple mission

Maya Reed is a certified 500-hour RYT instructor who has taught yoga across three continents before settling into her home studio. Her classes blend breathwork, mindful movement, and strength — designed to meet you exactly where you are, whether that's your very first downward dog or your thousandth.
